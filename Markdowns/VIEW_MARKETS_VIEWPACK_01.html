View Pack 01 — real per-occurrence studies + modeled option legs

Every row is one historical occurrence (an event date, or a non-overlapping window). Equity returns are REAL (cached NIFTY-500 daily matrix, as-of 2026-06-30). Option-leg returns are MODELED with a Black-Scholes pricer on the underlying's realized move over the window, at the stated IV, held to expiry — so they show real convexity incl. −100% losses. Returns are per occurrence, never compounded across occurrences. Modeled ≠ a live-chain quote.

Honest caveats: (1) theme "occurrences" are non-overlapping calendar windows (no discrete event to key on) — Middle-East & Gold are the true event studies; (2) young constituents (Paytm/Kalyan/Sona '21+) shorten those baskets' history, so n is small; (3) option legs assume a flat IV and hold-to-expiry — a real deploy prices the live chain. Weak edges (power, fintech) are shown as-is, not dressed up.
