Every trader marks up the same chart by hand, then guesses whether the lines hold. Pivot measures instead. Name an instrument, and ask in plain language. It draws only what it detected: a falling wedge, a rounding bottom, a double bottom, each with its dates and its neckline. Then ask the harder question. Has this pattern ever actually worked? Pivot answers: no historical base rate available. It will not validate its own drawing. Ask why a session moved, and it tells you what it did not show. No catalyst. No level crossed. No measurable edge. Nothing is drawn that wasn't computed. Pivot. The analyst at your chart.
